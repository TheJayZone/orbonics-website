About Orbonics

Game Development Studio

Unofficially founded over thirty years ago by two college students, Orbonics is a mis-spelling of the the word 'Aubonics' which is a type of advanced martial arts. Officially founded in 2025, Orbonics is now an independent game development studio passionate about creating innovative gaming experiences. Our diverse team of developers, artists, 3D modellers, audio engineers and designers work together to push the boundaries of interactive entertainment. At the moment, we are primarily focused on reviving old classics, faithfully keeping the original feel and gameplay. If nostalia is your thing, you can rely on Orbonics.
