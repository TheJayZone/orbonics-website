## 2. About the App

Music Player is an Android application designed to play audio files stored on your device.

Its features include:

- Selecting one or more music folders using the Android system folder picker.
- Browsing selected folders and their subfolders.
- Playing supported audio files, including MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, OGA, Opus, FLAC, WAV and WebM.
- Continuous and gapless playback where supported by the audio format and device.
- Reading available music metadata, including track titles, artists and album information.
- Displaying embedded or locally stored album artwork.
- Background playback and Android media notification controls.
- Playback controls through compatible Bluetooth devices, lock screens and voice assistants.
- Organising and reordering selected music folders.

The App is intended for personal music playback. It does not provide, sell or distribute music.
