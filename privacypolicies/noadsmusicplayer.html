Privacy Policy

No Ads Music Player for Android

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version

No Ads Music Player plays the music files already on your device, in the folders you choose. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no connection to any online service. It does not collect any personal data of any kind, and it never sends anything — of any kind — to anyone.

The app does not even request the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending any data off your device.

What we do not do

The app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in,
  • collect or send usage, analytics or crash data,
  • display advertising or use your activity for advertising,
  • read your location, contacts or any other device data,
  • make any network connection at all.

What the app accesses on your device

The only things the app touches are on your own device:

  • Music files in folders you pick. You choose which folders to open using Android's folder picker, and the app can read only the folders you explicitly grant it access to. No storage permissions are requested, and you can revoke access to any folder at any time in the system settings.
  • Tags inside your audio files. The app reads the metadata embedded in your music files (title, artist, album, artwork) so it can display them. This happens entirely on your device; your files are never copied, altered or moved.
  • The app's own settings. Your chosen folders, playback position and a small cache of file tags are stored on your device only, so the app remembers where you left off. If Android's app backup is enabled for this app, that information may be included in your own private backup; it is never sent to us.

Nothing is stored on any server, and nothing is visible to us.

Permissions

The app uses the following Android permissions, all for local playback:

Permission Why it is used
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows the media notification with playback controls on the lock screen and via Bluetooth (Android 13 and later). If you decline, the app still plays music, just without that notification.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Keeps playback running in the background when you leave the app or the screen turns off.
WAKE_LOCK Keeps the device awake at the moment one track hands over to the next, so gapless playback is not interrupted.

No storage, location or other data-collecting permissions are used.

Sharing with third parties

The app shares no data with anyone or anything: no developers, no service providers, no advertisers, no analytics tools, no other apps. There are no third-party components in the app that communicate with external services.

Children

No Ads Music Player is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy. Any significant change will be reflected by a new "last updated" date on this page and, where appropriate, by a note in the app's release notes on Google Play.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how the app handles your data, contact us at support@orbonics.uk.
